PRE-LAUNCH INFORMATION

Privacy notice

Updated: 26 November 2026. This provisional notice describes the product website and request-only demonstration. It is not the customer data-processing agreement or production privacy policy required before processing real academy data.

Information we collect

When you submit a demo or founding-pilot request, we ask for your name, business email address, academy name, region, organisation type and optional operational priorities. We use this information to assess the request, respond to you and manually grant or decline access. A submission does not create a user account or automatically approve access.

Where requests are stored

Submissions are sent securely to our Render-hosted demonstration API and stored in a separate Neon PostgreSQL staging database. The service records a pseudonymised technical rate-limiting identifier and the time of submission. Approved invitations use a one-time link with an expiring, browser-bound session; approval status and activation times are recorded. Website hosting and API providers may also hold ordinary access logs.

Purpose and access

We use requests only for the demo or founding-pilot enquiry and related replies. Access is restricted to authorised review personnel. We do not operate behavioural marketing pixels on this site. No payment is requested through this form.

Retention and requests

We will remove rejected, inactive and stale applications under a defined data-retention process before wider launch. To request access, correction or deletion of your enquiry, contact the address from which our team replies. A permanent public privacy contact and formal retention schedule remain launch requirements.

Demonstration safety

The Karate Masters OS demo uses fictional student profiles, fees and event data. Do not enter identifiable child data, payment details, real telephone numbers or private customer information into the demo. Approval is temporary and may be revoked.

Production launch

Before accepting real academy, parent or student data, we must publish independently reviewed privacy terms covering data roles, guardian consent, cross-border processing, security, rights requests, retention and deletion. This short pre-launch notice does not replace those obligations.
